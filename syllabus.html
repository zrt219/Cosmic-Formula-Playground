PHYSICAL MEANING
📄

The big picture (ELI5) ✨

The universe is expanding, so galaxies farther away move away from us — like dots on a balloon getting farther apart. But galaxies also feel gravity from nearby matter, which can pull them off their smooth expansion. We observe a mix of both effects: the Hubble flow plus a peculiar velocity.

💡

Galaxies follow the expansion, but gravity still has a say!

📄

Worked example

A galaxy is 100 Mpc away with a peculiar velocity of −500 km/s.

Hubble flow: vH = H0 d = 70 × 100 = 7,000 km/s
Peculiar velocity: vpec = −500 km/s (toward us)
Observed velocity: cz = vH + vpec = 7,000 − 500 = 6,500 km/s
✓ So we measure cz = 6,500 km/s, which is smaller than the Hubble flow alone due to the local tug.
